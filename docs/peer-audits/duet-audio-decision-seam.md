## How DUET makes the choice

DUET warms Whisper in `setup()` before the scenario clock, acknowledges an ended audio turn, and transcribes each chunk in a background task. It joins results by arrival index and waits for every chunk through the end marker before planning, so a fast first-chunk result cannot win over a later correction ([runtime](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/duet/runtime.py#L120-L137), [audio assembly](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/duet/runtime.py#L239-L304)).

- **Public audio 05:** the CPU `base` transcript yields no city; the turbo transcript's possible “Austin” is about 0.47, below DUET's 0.65 slot-confidence action gate. Either way it asks about the city (by name when a weak candidate exists) and does not search before the user's 4.2-second confirmation turn. The confirmed “Boston” is clear enough to call `flight_search` ([ASR notes](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/duet/perception/asr.py#L1-L31), [thresholds](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/duet/config.py#L104-L142), [case checkpoints](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/scenarios/pub_05_audio_asr_ambiguity.json#L30-L59)).
- **Public audio 06:** DUET waits for the end marker, merges the chunks, and applies the correction to the extracted slot. Even though the whole-turn confidence can be low, the corrected “New York” slot words are clear enough; it searches New York and never searches the abandoned Boston ([slot gate](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/duet/runtime.py#L326-L383), [two-pass repair](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/duet/planner/rules.py#L380-L419), [case checkpoints](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/scenarios/pub_06_audio_disfluency.json#L30-L58)).

DUET uses slot-word probabilities when available, falls back to utterance confidence when a backend has no word scores, and compares competing hypotheses. The timing contract gives 800 ms for full latency credit and zero by 2.5 s per scored response; audio 05 separately requires clarification before the 4.2 s second turn. Scenario-end work has a 4.2 s flush budget inside the 6 s tail ([protocol](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/docs/PROTOCOL.md#L120-L123), [timing config](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/duet/config.py#L83-L102)).

## What transfers to THREAD

THREAD's opt-in adapter returns transcript text and source evidence but no word scores, and the planner marks a successful local transcript `uncertain: false` ([adapter output](../../participant/local_asr.py#L184-L212), [local observation](../../participant/planner.py#L1399-L1437)). The independent acoustic task and Gemini planning task run concurrently, but after acoustic completion the planner still awaits Gemini; Gemini timeout raises a planning error ([parallel seam](../../participant/planner.py#L1219-L1256), [timeout path](../../participant/planner.py#L1000-L1035)). Thus a local decode under two seconds can still end in no plan when MAIN times out. The local feasibility measurement is warm ASR only, not end-to-end latency ([measurement and limits](local-whisper-feasibility.md#L70-L85)).

Generalizable improvement: let a **completed, source-current local transcript** feed the existing generic, manifest-validated read-only planner while Gemini runs. Require calibrated confidence for the task-critical slot and no unresolved competing value; otherwise clarify. Assemble all chunks in order and apply self-repair before any search. This could let a clear, safe `flight_search` proceed without waiting for MAIN, while keeping Gemini for complex plans and preventing local ASR from authorizing writes. Do not transplant DUET's numeric thresholds; THREAD's adapter currently exposes no word probabilities to calibrate.

## Public-fit and hidden-test risks

I found no production branch on `pub_05`/`pub_06` IDs or audio filenames. DUET does explicitly tune its confidence thresholds against those public clips; the city table in `agent.py` belongs to the marked, non-submission `BaselineAgent`, not `ParticipantAgent` ([entry point and baseline boundary](https://github.com/tannnmayy/DUET-SAMSUNGPRISM-HACKATHON-TEAM-SE7EN/blob/4d08c2b0c9a0a29e39559fb7b2439029aff5725d/agent/agent.py#L24-L47)). Hidden tests should vary cities, accents, noise, model/backend confidence, and alternative hypotheses; include a high-confidence first chunk later retracted, out-of-order or late chunks, missing end markers, source changes/cancellation, and non-read-only or absent-tool cases. Assert no search for an abandoned slot and no action when confidence is absent or ambiguous.
