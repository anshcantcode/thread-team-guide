# THREAD — Keep the thread.

An original product film in landscape, plus a separately composed vertical cut.
Direction: tactile acoustic glass, ink, electric blue, considered typography and
purposeful camera moves. The interruption changes the edit itself. No feature
grid, stock dashboard animation, rainbow gradients or generic technology montage.

## Brand

Use the existing THREAD sphere and Manrope family. Ink `#080D14`, white
`#F5F7FC`, electric blue `#2378F3`, soft blue `#A9C7F5`. Keep generous negative
space, carefully placed highlights and a restrained wordmark.

## Story

1. An optical macro: conversation is not a straight line.
2. The sphere resolves; meet THREAD.
3. A flight request is interrupted: Delhi becomes Mumbai. Chennai, tomorrow and
   after 21:00 survive the correction. The old branch is cut. The music drops out
   on “Wait”, then the corrected branch lights up.
4. The mechanism becomes a human benefit: change your mind, keep the details.
5. Actual Galaxy S24 screenshots show the Android home, search, rich sports and
   home-screen widgets. These are photographed-in-motion product views.
6. THREAD now runs independently of the laptop on the phone.
7. A sonic signature and the invitation: Go on. Keep the thread.

## Truth in the film

- A product film, not an unedited latency benchmark. The flight sequence is an
  illustrated interaction and uses demo flight data; label it during the scene.
- The demonstrated engine supports corrections and retained constraints.
- Device screenshots come from actual project verification. Preserve their
  contents; display and perspective crops do not invent app features.
- On-phone backend is implemented; Gemini voice and fresh data require internet.
- No Spotify playback, offline AI, always-on wake word, autonomous purchases,
  official Samsung endorsement, unmeasured latency numbers or winning claims.

## Audio

Directed synthetic Charon narration; Aoede for the interruption character. Dry,
close vocal recording, gentle mastering, a composed original electronic score,
stereo motion accents, sub impacts and an intentional interruption dropout.
Keep speech intelligible on laptop speakers and phones. No borrowed commercial
music. Sidecar captions and separate sound stems accompany the finished exports.
